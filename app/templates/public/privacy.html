Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose & Philosophy

This Known Issues & Status service is built exclusively to keep smart home users informed about technical defects, firmware updates, and workarounds. We believe in strict data minimization and user privacy.

2. What Data We Store

When you subscribe to an issue, we store strictly what is technically necessary to deliver notifications:

  • Email address: Used solely to send transactional notifications for the specific issue you subscribed to.
  • Issue identifier: To know which issue you wish to monitor.
  • Subscription status: Pending confirmation, confirmed, or unsubscribed.
  • Cryptographic tokens: Secure random tokens used to verify double opt-in confirmation and facilitate one-click unsubscription.
  • Timestamps: When you requested, confirmed, or unsubscribed.

3. What We Never Do

  • We never use email tracking pixels or read receipts.
  • We never send marketing emails, advertisements, or newsletters.
  • We never sell, share, or rent your email address to any third party.
  • We do not use third-party analytics cookies or tracking scripts.

4. Double Opt-In & Unsubscribing

Subscriptions require double opt-in verification: you must confirm the activation link sent to your inbox before any future notifications are dispatched. Every email notification contains a direct, secure unsubscription link that immediately ceases all further communication for that issue.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR / AVG

You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data at any time. For self-hosted instances of this software, data is stored exclusively on the server administrator's infrastructure.
